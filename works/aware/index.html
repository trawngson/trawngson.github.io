AWARE

AWARE (AI Waste-sorting and Recycling Enhancement) is a mobile AI application utilizing the YOLO framework to provide real-time, offline waste classification and actionable recycling instructions directly from live video footage.

Computer Vision

Project Overview

AWARE

With assistance from: Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology
My Role: Full-stack Technical Developer

AWARE is a collaboration project that's been the most fun to work on, and it's also the one project I've dedicated the most into. From automated waste identification to novel recycling ideas residing in a small gallery within your palm, AWARE aims to make recycling feel effortless and push initiative in my community.
